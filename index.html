The Masterpiece

The LaFerrari represents the absolute height of Ferrari's technological prowess. Code-named F150, it merges naturally aspirated V12 performance with Formula 1-derived hybrid electrification (HY-KERS). The electric motor is integrated directly into the dual-clutch transmission to eliminate low-end torque gaps and offer instant throttle response.

Advanced Engineering & Features

HY-KERS Hybrid Power

Combines a ferocious 6.3-litre V12 engine revving up to 9,250 RPM with an electric motor derived directly from Formula 1 racing, unleashing a combined 950 horsepower seamlessly with zero turbo lag.

Active Aerodynamics

Features computer-controlled movable diffusers, guide vanes on the underbody, and a rear spoiler that automatically deploy depending on dynamic driving conditions to maximize high-speed downforce and cornering stability.

Carbon Monocoque Chassis

Hand-assembled using four different types of high-strength carbon fibre—same as the Scuderia Ferrari F1 single-seaters—delivering 27% more torsional rigidity and 22% more beam stiffness than its predecessor.

Brembo Carbon Ceramic Brakes

Equipped with lightweight, next-generation Brembo carbon-ceramic brake discs integrated with new composite materials that optimize cooling and deliver phenomenal stopping power under extreme track loads.

F1-Derived Dual Clutch

Utilizes a 7-speed dual-clutch transmission specifically calibrated to handle massive torque curves while executing lightning-fast gear shifts in milliseconds.

Track Telemetry & Cockpit

The interior features custom-molded fixed seats matched to the driver's exact posture, a steering wheel packed with integrated controls, and a fully digital configurable instrument display inspired by Scuderia Ferrari.
